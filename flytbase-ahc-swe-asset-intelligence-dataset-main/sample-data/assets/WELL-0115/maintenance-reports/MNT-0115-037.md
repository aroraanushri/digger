## What happened

Field staff reported that a damp, dark patch appeared along the flowline route. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0115-0207 showed the wellhead at 29.8 °C with the air at 25.1 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Excavation found a corrosion pinhole on the bottom of the line.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 16 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain is making the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Internal corrosion from produced water collecting at a low point.
