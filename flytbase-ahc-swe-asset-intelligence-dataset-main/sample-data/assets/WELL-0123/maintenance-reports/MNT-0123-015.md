## What happened

Field staff reported that the wellhead was cold and the pump jack was straining with a heavy, uneven load. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0123-0095 showed the wellhead at 27.8 °C with the air at 22.0 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The downhole pump was stuck, most likely from sand or scale.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 16 days longer than the repair itself because the workover rig is busy on other wells.

## Root cause

A worn plunger that let sand settle and lock the pump.
