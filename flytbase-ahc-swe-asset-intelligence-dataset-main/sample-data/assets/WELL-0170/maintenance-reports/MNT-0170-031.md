## What happened

Field staff reported that the pumper heard grinding from the walking beam and saw the horsehead wobbling. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0170-0202 showed the wellhead at 34.2 °C with the air at 32.0 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. A vibration check and visual inspection found play in the saddle bearing and scoring on the bearing housing.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 37 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part is back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Grease contaminated with sand had worn the bearing.

- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).
