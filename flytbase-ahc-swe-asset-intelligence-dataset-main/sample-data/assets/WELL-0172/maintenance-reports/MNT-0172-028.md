## What happened

Field staff reported that the inspector saw wet soil above the buried flowline. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0172-0204 showed the wellhead at 28.3 °C with the air at 25.4 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Excavation found a corrosion pinhole on the bottom of the line.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 17 days longer than the repair itself because the crew is committed to a higher-priority spill response elsewhere in the field.

## Root cause

Internal corrosion from produced water collecting at a low point.

- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).
